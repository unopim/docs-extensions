# Support

For any questions, reach out to us at [support@webkul.com](mailto:support@webkul.com) or raise a ticket through our [HelpDesk System](https://webkul.uvdesk.com/en/).

It helps if you can tell us:

- Which AI client you're using, and whether it connects locally or over the internet.
- Whether `php artisan mcp:inspector unopim-dev` connects and lists the tools.
- The exact error message you saw.
