# Support

Need help with the AI Product Feed extension? We're here for you.

- **Email:** [support@webkul.com](mailto:support@webkul.com)
- **HelpDesk:** raise a ticket through our [HelpDesk System](https://webkul.uvdesk.com/en/)

Before you write, check [Troubleshooting & FAQ](./troubleshooting.md). Most problems are solved there in a minute. If you contact us, please include:

1. Your UnoPim version and PHP version (`php -v`).
2. The failing row from **OpenAI Feed › General › View All** (status, error, triggered by).
3. The `[OpenAIFeed]` lines from `storage/logs/laravel.log` around the time of the failure.
4. Your queue driver (`QUEUE_CONNECTION` in `.env`) and whether the scheduler is running.

Please **never** send us your feed token, SFTP password or private key.
